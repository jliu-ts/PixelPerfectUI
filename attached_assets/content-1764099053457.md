F.A.Q.

- What is white label video editing?






White label video editing refers to video editing software that can be quickly adapted to the client’s branding. It lets companies quickly get the core technology they need for a fraction of the cost it would take to develop such features from scratch.

- What is white label customization?






White label customization is modifying specially designed off-the-shelf software (e.g. a white label video app) to fit the client’s corporate style. It might involve simple changes like color scheme and logos or something more advanced (e.g. new user flow, screen layout, etc.). Customization of the interface is the most common type of adjustment, although certain products allow changing the functionality as well.

The purpose of white label video editor is to provide advanced video editing capabilities in a fraction of the time it would take to develop them from scratch. Customizability lets such products seamlessly integrate with the clients’ apps.

- Is white label video editing legal?




White label video editing is perfectly legal, assuming you have purchased the editor itself. Changing its interface is part of the deal.
